# Our Technology


### ADVANCED VACUUM DISTILLATION {#advanced-vacuum-distillation .unnumbered}

**Precision Engineering for Zero-Degradation Refining**

Traditional continuous-flow recycling systems often compromise the molecular integrity of the oil through extreme heat and thermal cracking. At Orion Oil, we have rejected the standard approach. Our facility utilizes a highly specialized, custom-engineered batch-wise distillation architecture designed exclusively for the complex processing of waste engine and industrial oils.

By operating under extreme deep-vacuum conditions and utilizing a highly calibrated gas-fired thermal system, we achieve a level of fractional precision that ensures every drop of recovered base oil retains its consistent, high-purity re-refined performance characteristics.

**Core Technical Pillars of Our Architecture**

a.  **Extreme Deep-Vacuum Environment (1-2 Torr)** The cornerstone of our process is the artificial suppression of the oil's boiling point. By dropping the internal atmospheric pressure of our custom 5,000-liter batch kettles down to an absolute vacuum of 1 to 2 mm Hg (Torr), we evaporate heavy hydrocarbon chains at significantly lower temperatures.

-   **Zero Thermal Degradation:** Because the oil is never exposed to excessive, destructive heat, the hydrocarbon molecules are never burned or cracked during the heavy extraction phase.

-   **Odor & Impurity Reduction:** The deep-vacuum environment continuously draws out volatile contaminants and sulfur compounds, leaving a cleaner, highly stable distillate.

b.  **Dual-Stage Thermal Fluid Heating**

Consistent, controlled heat transfer is vital to molecular preservation. Rather than relying on direct firing, our plant utilizes an advanced, PID-controlled gas-fired thermic fluid heating system.

-   **Precision Heat Transfer:** We circulate specialized thermal fluids at exactly 350°C through a dual-heating matrix.

-   **Synchronized Infrastructure:** The kettle is equipped with an integrated limpet coil, working in perfect synchronization with a highly efficient shell-and-tube reboiler (featuring seamless SS 304 tubes). This ensures rapid, evenly distributed heat without localized hot spots that could damage the oil.

c.  **Meticulous Batch-Wise Precision** We prioritize absolute control over speed. Our system is engineered to process high-capacity 4,000-liter batches over a precisely timed 8-hour cycle.

-   **Fractional Extraction:** This batch-wise approach allows us to strictly monitor and isolate distinct molecular cuts at different time intervals---seamlessly separating moisture, extracting our Industrial LDO at 5 mm Hg, and finally capturing the Premium Base Oil during the extended deep-vacuum phase.

-   **Heavy Bottoms Recovery:** The remaining uncracked residue is easily captured at the end of the batch, yielding our dense, high-performance Asphalt Modifier.

#### **The Result: Uncompromised Molecular Integrity** {#the-result-uncompromised-molecular-integrity .unnumbered}

Our advanced vacuum distillation technology guarantees that the base oil we recover retains the characteristic molecular structure of the original feedstock. For lubricant blenders and industrial manufacturers, this means access to a highly refined, naturally high-viscosity product with exceptional oxidation stability---all while actively supporting a closed-loop, zero-waste circular economy.

### OUR REFINING PROCESS {#our-refining-process .unnumbered}

**Engineered for Zero-Waste & Maximum Yield**

**1. Pre-Treatment & Dehydration** The refining cycle begins by loading a 4,000-liter batch of specialized waste engine and industrial oil into our 5 KL gross capacity kettle. During this initial stage, the feedstock is strictly preheated for one hour. Once the exact temperature is reached, we apply a 70 mm Hg absolute vacuum for one hour to safely evaporate and extract all water content. This complete dehydration stabilizes the oil for the extreme thermal processing ahead.

**2. Deep Vacuum Distillation** The dehydrated oil is subjected to advanced fractional distillation utilizing a precision-controlled, gas-fired thermic fluid heating system operating at 350°C. We first extract the light diesel and LDO cuts under a 5 mm Hg vacuum. The system pressure is then dropped to a deep 1 to 2 mm Hg absolute vacuum for an extended 5-hour evaporation phase. This extreme vacuum artificially lowers the boiling point, allowing us to safely extract up to 70% of the premium heavy oil fractions (approx. 2,520 kg per batch) without any thermal degradation or molecular cracking.

**3. Chemical Polishing** Distillation is followed by a rigorous, continuous purification phase utilizing our specialized oil polishing skid, engineered to process 4.8 Tons every 24 hours. The distilled oil undergoes forced percolation through a 20-column matrix loaded with a specialized, highly reactive Alumina Bauxite blend. This advanced percolation neutralizes total acid numbers (TAN), extracts microscopic sludge, and dramatically restores the oil's visual clarity, achieving an ASTM color rating of \~3 in just a single pass.

**4. Final Testing & Quality Check**

Every batch is held to uncompromising industrial standards before it leaves our facility. Samples are pulled directly from the polishing discharge and analyzed by a NABL accredited Third Party Inspection (TPI) laboratory. Utilizing advanced chemical analysis instrumentation---including Gas Chromatography, FTIR, and AAS---we verify that the kinematic viscosity, flash point, copper corrosion, and specific gravity perfectly match our strict internal baselines and exact customer requirements.

**5. Premium Base Oil & By-Products** Our batch-wise architecture is a true closed-loop, zero-waste system. The primary yield is our flagship Premium Base Oil (SN-150 and SN-300 equivalents), restored to a consistent, high-purity re-refined state. Simultaneously, we capture 100% of the by-products for industrial use: the light ends are condensed into our high-energy Industrial LDO, while the remaining 720 kg of heavy, uncracked residue is safely discharged from the kettle to serve as a high-viscosity Asphalt Modifier for road and infrastructure applications.
